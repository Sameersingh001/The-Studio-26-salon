OUR PHILOSOPHY

Uncompromising Hygiene

Your safety and comfort are our topmost priorities.

At The Studio 26, we follow strict international hygiene protocols. Every tool is carefully sterilized, and single-use disposables are used wherever possible to ensure 100% cleanliness.

Step into our beautifully designed, pristine salon environment where you can truly relax, knowing you are in the safest hands in Agra.

OUR JOURNEY

A Passion for Beauty

The Studio 26 Salon was founded with a singular vision: to create a space where beauty, comfort, and luxury coexist. We started as a small team of passionate artists and have grown into Agra's most trusted name in bridal and beauty services.

Our journey is fueled by the smiles of our satisfied clients. Every haircut, every bridal transformation, and every glowing face is a testament to our dedication. We don't just transform looks; we uplift confidence.
